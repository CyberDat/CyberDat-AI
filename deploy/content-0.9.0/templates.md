# English editorial templates — version 1

Every template uses the same machine-readable fields. The following editorial objectives are for a future separately authorized real adapter, not claims about mock output.

| Template | Editorial focus | Required distinctions |
|---|---|---|
| Cybersecurity News | What happened, when, affected parties, what is confirmed | Publication date versus event date; allegation versus confirmed fact; vendor statement versus independent reporting |
| Threat Intelligence Analysis | Actor/campaign context, observed behavior, defensive implications | Attribution confidence; observed behavior versus inference; source provenance and reporting limits |
| Vulnerability Analysis | Affected versions/products, CVE, confirmed severity and mitigations | CVSS version/source; confirmed KEV membership versus exploit probability; patch guidance versus untested workaround |
| Scientific Research Review | Research question, method, findings, limitations and reproducibility | Preprint versus peer-reviewed status; author claim versus validated result; benchmarks versus deployment evidence |
| AI Security Analysis | AI-specific threat model, attack/defense assumptions and practical limits | Model/version/test context; demonstrated result versus hypothetical risk; measured behavior versus speculation |

Required document sections:

1. **Title:** accurate scope, no unsupported urgency or superlatives.
2. **Summary:** concise, source-grounded English overview.
3. **Confirmed facts:** individual assertions with supplied source IDs. Human review must verify entailment; an ID alone is insufficient.
4. **Technical analysis:** distinguish explanation and editorial inference from observed facts. State prerequisites and missing details.
5. **Practical recommendations:** proportionate, sourced, dated and applicable to the stated versions. Do not invent vendor guidance or execute exploits.
6. **Sources:** primary references first, preserve canonical HTTPS URLs, publisher and source publication dates from the evidence brief.
7. **Uncertainties and limitations:** missing evidence, unresolved claims, partial coverage, method/benchmark limits and review status.

No fabricated quotes. Stage 1 quotations are disabled. A later quoting workflow requires exact verified excerpts, attribution, source location and copyright limits; it cannot infer a quote from a summary. No template triggers publication or substitutes a mock draft for a real article.
